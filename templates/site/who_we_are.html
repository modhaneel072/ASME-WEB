About Us

Who We Are

The University of Iowa ASME student chapter is a community of engineers who design, build, and compete together. We turn classroom knowledge into hands-on experience.

Our Mission

To foster a community of mechanical engineering students at the University of Iowa who grow through hands-on design, competitive teamwork, and professional development. We equip members with real-world engineering skills and a network that lasts beyond college.

Why ASME

More Than a Club

ASME at Iowa gives you access to real engineering projects, professional connections, and the tools to build things that matter.

⚙

Hands-On Projects

Design, prototype, and compete in national ASME competitions. Work on real engineering challenges with real deadlines.

⚡

3D Print Lab

Free access to Bambu Lab printers for coursework, personal projects, and competition parts. Submit prints through our portal.

🎓

Professional Growth

Industry speakers, resume workshops, networking events, and connections to engineering companies hiring Iowa grads.

🛠

Tool & Equipment Library

Borrow tools, measurement equipment, and prototyping supplies from our inventory system. Check out with NFC or through the portal.

🚀

Competition Teams

Join our drone racing, robotics, or design competition teams. Travel to regionals and nationals representing Iowa.

🤝

Community

Social events, study groups, cookouts, and a network of like-minded engineers. The people you build with become your network.

What We Do

Three Pillars

⚙

Design & Build

We design, manufacture, and test real engineering systems. From drones to rovers, our teams take projects from concept to competition.

📈

Testing & Validation

Our projects go through rigorous testing cycles. Members learn to iterate, measure, and improve designs using real engineering processes.

👥

Leadership & Collaboration

Run meetings, manage budgets, coordinate teams, and present to stakeholders. ASME builds leaders, not just engineers.

What Members Gain

  • Hands-on experience with CAD, manufacturing, electronics, and systems integration
  • Free access to 3D printers and prototyping tools through our inventory system
  • Travel to regional and national ASME competitions
  • Resume-building project leadership and team management experience
  • Professional networking with industry engineers and Iowa alumni
  • A community of motivated, like-minded engineering students

About ASME

  • ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) is a global professional organization founded in 1880
  • Over 85,000 members across 140+ countries in industry, academia, and government
  • ASME sets engineering standards used worldwide in design, manufacturing, and safety
  • Student chapters connect university engineers to the broader professional community
