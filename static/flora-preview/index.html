About

Who We Are

ASME at the University of Iowa is a student organization for engineers who want to learn by doing. We bring together students interested in mechanical design, manufacturing, teamwork, and professional growth through projects, events, workshops, and competition-focused work.

Hands-On Projects

Move from concept to prototype through real engineering work, iteration, and team problem-solving.

Professional Growth

Develop communication, leadership, and technical confidence through chapter events and collaboration.

Fabrication Experience

Learn how ideas become physical builds through tools, materials, and practical workflows.

Engineering Community

Join a team-oriented environment where students support one another and build together.
